# Decisions by area

This file lists the decisions in force that shape the futures book, by area. Each row says what the decision does to
the book, the economic reason it fits a 500,000 account that trades whole contracts (tied to Carver's Advanced Futures
Trading Strategies, AFTS, where it applies), and the measured support, with the book and window it was measured on. A
rejected idea is listed only where it explains why something is not done. Decisions since replaced are left out; the
ones that matter for reading older material are in [CARVER_DEPARTURES](CARVER_DEPARTURES.md), section "Superseded
decisions to know".

The long forms are elsewhere, and each area names its sections: every departure from Carver in
[CARVER_DEPARTURES](CARVER_DEPARTURES.md); how the book is set in [THE_BOOK](../01_the_book/THE_BOOK.md); the roll rule
and the data in [ROLLS_AND_DATA](../01_the_book/ROLLS_AND_DATA.md); the mechanics in the tracked documents
([TREND_FOLLOWING_SYSTEM](../../TREND_FOLLOWING_SYSTEM.md), [OPTIMIZER_AND_RISK_DESIGN](../../OPTIMIZER_AND_RISK_DESIGN.md),
[RISK_MODULES](../../RISK_MODULES.md), [COST_MODEL](../../COST_MODEL.md), [FUTURES_ROLLS](../../FUTURES_ROLLS.md) and
[DATA_SOURCES_OF_TRUTH](../../DATA_SOURCES_OF_TRUTH.md)). The equity book is in
[THE_EQUITY_BOOK](../01_the_book/THE_EQUITY_BOOK.md). How the books are run, stored and reported is in
[RUNBOOKS](../05_operations/RUNBOOKS.md), [LIVE_RUN_CYCLE](../../LIVE_RUN_CYCLE.md) and THE_BOOK, sections "Run modes and
the book e-mail" and "What is stored"; the QT desk's book is in the QT design documents under `docs/design/`.

Code citations are paths under `services/trade-ngin/` in AlgoGators/algogators at commit 4c92eb1b8d, main's merge of
`AlgoGators/algogators#98`, which imports trade-ngin d6f998da.
Commit ids are those of AlgoGators/algogators, whose history was rebuilt at the import; each is given beside the
AlgoGators/trade-ngin pull request that carried it, which is where to look for it in the trade-ngin repository.

## How to read this

- **Columns.** Date, Decision (what it does to the book), Why (the economics, with Carver's basis), Data (the measured
  support, or what the code does where nothing was measured).
- **Names.** The chosen setup is the book of record, picked on 2026-10-29 and carried by the conservative template
  (section 9). The starting book of the first tuning round is the book of 2026-10-26, with full-size gold and silver and
  the October limits. The gold and silver micro book is the granularity buffer with gold and silver through MGC and SIL,
  the setup of 2026-10-27 and the base of the final grid. A reference model run outside the engine is an independent
  Python model of the loop design, used to measure rules before the engine was built to them.
- **Numbers.** Sharpe is the account Sharpe after costs unless a row says otherwise. The tuning windows are 16 years, 10,
  5, 2 and 1, ending at the data end; the 10y, 5y, 2y and year run from five start dates each, and "the mean" is the
  mean over the five windows. Round trips are counted a year.
- **Options tested and not shipped** are kept, readable and re-runnable, on the archive branches
  `archive/keep-do-not-delete/s25-one-pass`, `archive/keep-do-not-delete/step-edge`, `archive/keep-do-not-delete/levers`,
  `archive/keep-do-not-delete/futures-integration` and `archive/keep-do-not-delete/main-rolls-before-rewrite` on
  AlgoGators/trade-ngin.

## 5. Optimiser

The one pass, its starting point, the buffer and the optimiser covariance. Long forms: [CARVER_DEPARTURES](CARVER_DEPARTURES.md),
section "Dynamic optimisation (strategy twenty-five)" and its subsections, and section "No separate per-instrument

| Date | Decision | Why | Data |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-30 and 10-01 | One pass, every day: the overlay reads the unrounded target and applies its multiplier once, the whole-contract search runs once, and nothing feeds back. Leverage limits are not constraints inside the search. A rounded book still over a hard limit is trimmed one contract at a time, at most five a day. Weekly rebalancing is not used. | Carver applies the risk multiplier once to the unrounded positions, before the optimiser (AFTS, tactic four, p.612; footnote 293, p.622), so no cut is left to deliver after rounding; the trim restores a limit that whole contracts broke with the fewest contracts. With a buffer already holding churn down, a weekly cadence would leave the book on stale signals and stale risk for days. | The earlier lap loop, which iterated the overlay and the search, compounded cuts up to 5 times and went blind on 73 of 119 five-lap days; limits as constraints in the search gave more churn than the book had. | 2026-09-27 | The greedy search starts from the held book. | Carver's cost penalty already prices each trade against the contracts held (AFTS, strategy twenty-five, p.428), so the two starts price the same trades and differ in where the greedy path stops. From zero, on a lumpy book two nearly equal answers lie close together, small changes in the inputs send the path to one or the other on successive days, and the book rebuilds itself; from the held book a holding stays unless one contract's step lowers the objective. | On a reference model run outside the engine: 187 reversal pairs a year from the held book against 320 from zero, with tracking error 0.047 against 0.059.
